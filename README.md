### 🚀 Bike Purchase Analytics Dashboard

An interactive and professional Excel dashboard analyzing customer demographic and purchasing behavior across 1,000 customer records. 

#### 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Customers:** 1,000
* **Total Bikes Sold:** 481
* **Overall Conversion Rate:** 48.10%
* **Average Customer Income:** $56,360.00

#### 📈 Key Insights & Analytical Dimensions
* **Regional Performance:** The **Pacific** region leads with the highest conversion rate (58.85%), while **North America** represents the largest customer volume (508 customers).
* **Demographics & Education:** Customers with **Bachelors** and **Graduate Degrees** exhibit the highest purchase affinity (55.2% and 54.0% conversion rates respectively).
* **Core Buying Group:** **Middle Age** customers (ages 31–50) form the primary market driver, accounting for 701 customers with a **54.6%** purchase rate.
* **Commute Utility:** Shorter commute distances (0–1 miles and 2–5 miles) show significantly higher conversion rates, pointing toward practical commuter adoption.

#### 🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used
* **Microsoft Excel:** Pivot Tables, Advanced Formulas (`COUNTIFS`, `AVERAGEIF`, Binary Helper Columns), Interactive Slicers, and Dynamic Report Connections.
* **Data Visualization:** Clustered Column Charts, Horizontal Bar Charts, Donut Charts, and Custom KPI Cards.
